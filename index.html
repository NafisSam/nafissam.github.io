About

A physician-engineer, on purpose

I'm not leaving medicine to become an engineer — I'm building the bridge between them. My path combines clinical training with AI, engineering, and healthcare product development, because the problems I care most about (diagnosis, access, decision-making at the bedside) don't get solved by choosing just one side of that line.

The long-term goal is bigger than any single project: to build an organization that helps healthcare systems adopt AI responsibly — real pipelines, real evaluation, real clinical accountability — and leave the systems I work in a little better than I found them.

Day to day, that means moving between clinical rotations, research, teaching and mentoring junior researchers, and building software — usually in intense, focused cycles rather than evenly spread days.
