Manuscript Review Tool

Paste your manuscript, or upload a .txt/.md/.docx/.pdf file. The free review below runs deterministic, rule-based checks — structure, reporting-guideline checklists, cross-section consistency, citation matching — entirely in your browser, no AI, nothing uploaded to a server. An optional deep AI review is available further down if you want genuine research-level judgment, using your own API key.

This determines which reporting guideline gets checked (PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-ScR, SANRA, CONSORT, STROBE, STARD...) on top of the checklist items that apply to every manuscript.

.txt / .md read instantly. .docx / .pdf load a small parsing library from a CDN on first use — your document's content itself is never uploaded anywhere; parsing happens locally.
